Your first visit

What happens when you come in.

01

A proper conversation

We ask about your teeth, your health and anything that worries you. Nervous patients can ask for a longer slot.

02

A full exam

Teeth, gums, bite and a mouth cancer check, with x-rays only when they are needed.

03

A written plan

If you need treatment, you take home a plan with the options and an itemised price.

04

Time to decide

There is no pressure to book on the day. Your estimate stays valid for 90 days.
